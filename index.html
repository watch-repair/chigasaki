A Craftsman's Dedication 職人のこだわり

Nestled inside Yamada Denki LABI LIFE SELECT Chigasaki, Kawabata's has been a trusted name in vintage quartz watch repair for over two decades. Mr. Kawabata brings a meticulous eye and steady hand to every timepiece that crosses his workbench.

Specializing in iconic Japanese brands — Seiko, Citizen, Casio, and Orient — we understand that these watches are more than instruments. They are heirlooms, memories, and stories waiting to be told again.

From the delicate circuitry of early 1970s quartz movements to the intricate gearing of hybrid models, no mechanism is too obscure, no repair too delicate.

"Every tick restored is a memory reclaimed."

Our Services サービス内容

Comprehensive care for your treasured timepieces, delivered with precision and respect.

🔋

Battery Replacement 電池交換

Genuine or high-quality compatible batteries installed with careful case resealing and water-resistance testing. We preserve original gaskets whenever possible.

🔧

Movement Cleaning ムーブメント洗浄

Ultrasonic cleaning of quartz modules, gear trains, and circuit boards. Corrosion treatment and contact restoration for reliable long-term performance.

⏰

Crown & Pusher Repair リューズ・プッシャー修理

Stem replacement, crown threading repair, and pusher restoration for chronographs and digital-analog hybrids. Sourcing rare OEM parts when available.

📦

Vintage Parts Sourcing ヴィンテージ部材調達

Extensive network of domestic and international suppliers to locate discontinued crystals, circuit boards, coils, and case components for rare models.

💧

Water-Resistant Gasket Replacement 防水パッキン交換

Replacement of case-back, crown, and crystal gaskets to restore original water resistance. Pressure testing included for peace of mind.

🔄

Watch Band Replacement 時計バンド交換

Wide selection of leather, rubber, nylon, and metal bands to match your vintage piece. Professional fitting and lug-width matching included.

📏

Metal Band Adjustment メタルバンド調整

Precision resizing of stainless steel, titanium, and gold-filled bracelets. Pin, screw, and folded-link adjustments handled with care.

📝

Overhaul Estimate オーバーホール見積もり

Detailed inspection and transparent quotation for full movement overhaul, case refinishing, and dial restoration. No hidden costs, no pressure.

Frequently Asked Questions よくあるご質問

Quick answers to common questions about our watch repair services.

How long does a typical battery replacement take? 電池交換はどのくらいの時間がかかりますか？

Most battery replacements are completed within 15–30 minutes while you wait. We use genuine or high-quality compatible batteries and carefully reseal the case to maintain water resistance. For vintage models requiring special battery types, sourcing may take 1–3 business days. 電池交換の多くは、その場で15～30分で完了します。純正または高品質な互換電池を使用し、防水性を保つよう丁寧にケースを閉めます。特殊な電池タイプが必要なヴィンテージモデルの場合、部材調達に1～3営業日かかる場合があります。

Do I need an appointment for a repair consultation? 修理のご相談は予約が必要ですか？

No appointment is necessary for battery replacements or quick diagnostics. For complex repairs, overhauls, or vintage parts sourcing consultations, we recommend calling ahead or visiting during less busy hours (weekday mornings) to ensure Mr. Kawabata can dedicate sufficient time to assess your timepiece. 電池交換や簡易診断は予約不要です。複雑な修理、オーバーホール、ヴィンテージ部材の調達相談については、事前にお電話いただくか、比較的落ち着いた平日の午前中にご来店いただくことをお勧めします。川端氏が十分な時間をかけて時計を診察できるよう、スムーズなご対応を心がけています。

Which watch brands do you specialize in? どのブランドの時計を専門に修理していますか？

We specialize in iconic Japanese brands including Seiko, Citizen, Casio, and Orient. Our expertise covers vintage quartz movements from the early 1970s through modern hybrid models. We also service select Swiss and European quartz watches on a case-by-case basis. セイコー、シチズン、カシオ、オリエントなど、日本を代表するブランドを専門としています。1970年代初期のヴィンテージクォーツムーブメントから最新のハイブリッドモデルまで幅広く対応しており、一部のスイス・欧州製クォーツ時計についても、状況に応じて修理を承っております。

How much does a typical repair cost? 修理費用の目安を教えてください。

Battery replacements start from ¥1,500–¥3,500 depending on the model. Movement cleaning and basic servicing typically ranges from ¥8,000–¥20,000. For overhauls and complex restorations, we provide a detailed, transparent estimate after inspection — with no hidden costs and no pressure to proceed. 電池交換はモデルにより1,500円～3,500円から承っております。ムーブメント洗浄や基本整備は8,000円～20,000円程度が目安です。オーバーホールや複雑な修復作業については、検診後に詳細で透明性のあるお見積もりを提示いたします。隠れた費用は一切ございません。

Can you source parts for discontinued or rare watch models? 廃盤や希少なモデルの部品も調達できますか？

Yes. Through our extensive network of domestic and international suppliers, we can locate discontinued crystals, circuit boards, coils, case components, and other rare parts. While some parts may take several weeks to source, we keep you informed throughout the process and discuss alternatives if original parts are unavailable. はい、国内外の部材サプライヤーとの幅広いネットワークを活かし、廃盤となった風防、回路基板、コイル、ケース部品などの希少部材を調達することが可能です。一部の部品は調達に数週間を要する場合がありますが、プロセスの進捗を随時ご連絡し、純正品が入手できない場合は代替案についてもご相談いたします。

Where are you located and what are your business hours? 所在地と営業時間を教えてください。

We are located inside Yamada Denki LABI LIFE SELECT Chigasaki on the 1st floor south watch repair counter. We are open Monday, Wednesday through Sunday from 10:00 to 19:00. We are closed on Tuesdays. Hours may vary during New Year and Obon holidays — please call ahead to confirm. 山田電機 LABI LIFE SELECT 茅ヶ崎店 1F 南 時計修理フロアにて営業しております。営業時間は月曜日、水曜日から日曜日の10:00～19:00です。火曜日は定休日となります。年末年始・お盆期間は営業時間が異なる場合がありますので、事前にお電話でご確認ください。
